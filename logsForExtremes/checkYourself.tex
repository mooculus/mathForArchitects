    \item The number of deaths was increasing by the same percentage each day.

\begin{exercise}
    The decibel system is a log-scale for measuring sound volume. 
    A doubling of sound intensity represents an increase of approximately
$3$ decibels. Led Zeppelin once had a show rated at $130$ decibels. On the otherhand, \textit{Kiss} 
once had a show rated at $136$ decibels. How much more intense was the volume at the \textit{Kiss} show than the
\textit{Led Zeppelin} show? 
\end{exercise}

\answerlistbox{$10$}{(e), (g), (i)}{(a)}{(b), (c), (e)}{Four times more intense}
